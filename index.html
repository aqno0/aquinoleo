03 / ACADEMIC FOUNDATIONS

Digital Media Education & Pedagogy

Bridging technical precision with creative expression in digital media.

At Santa Monica College, my academic focus centers on digital media education—understanding not just how to create compelling visual assets, but how digital tools shape learning, communication, and visual storytelling.

By stripping away visual clutter and anchoring work in high-contrast monochrome and electric accents, we teach students and audiences to see composition, contour, and hierarchy with clarity. Digital media is both an expressive craft and a pedagogical vehicle.
